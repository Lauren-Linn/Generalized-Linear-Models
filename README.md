# Generalized Linear Models

## Introduction

Welcome to the Generalized Linear Models (GLM) repository. This repository contains solutions to three assignments completed during my Master's degree at Macquarie University. The assignments are designed to test and apply skills in both applied statistics and intermediate mathematical statistics. Each assignment focuses on different aspects of GLMs, incorporating a variety of datasets and theoretical questions related to statistical modeling and analysis.

## Assignments Overview

### Assignment 1: 

1. **Question 1:**  
   I was provided data from a study conducted by David Altman in 1991, which investigates lung function in cystic fibrosis patients aged 7-23 years. The dataset contains several variables, and it focuses on understanding the relationship between the response variable 'Maximum expiratory pressuree' and multiple explanatory variables.

2. **Question 2:**  
   Show that the Inverse Gaussian distribution is a member of the exponential family of distributions. 

3. **Question 3:**  
   Demonstrate that the maximum likelihood estimator of the parameter β is also the solution of the squared error loss (also known as least squares error).

### Assignment 2:

1. **Question 1:**  
   The dataset `pbc.csv` (a subset of `pbc2.id` from the R package JM) contains baseline data on 312 patients with primary biliary cirrhosis. The outcome of interest is serum bilirubin, a strong indicator of disease progression. Analyze the relationship between serum bilirubin and other variables, given that high levels are undesirable.

2. **Question 2:**  
   The dataset provides data on the number of emergency room visits by 788 health insurance subscribers. Build and evaluate the best statistical model to describe the relationship between emergency room visits and health insurance subscribers.

3. **Question 3:**  
   Show that the standard logistic distribution of εi is symmetric about 0.

### Assignment 3: 

1. **Question 1:**  
   The dataset contains information on the number of doctor visits for a sample of German male individuals in 1994. This data is a subsample of the German Socioeconomic Panel (SOEP). Decide and evaluate which statistical models are appropriate for analyzing the number of doctor visits in the last three months.

2. **Question 2:**  
   In a road safety experiment, cars with dummies in the driver or front passenger seat were crashed into a wall at 56 km/h. Analyze how the crash affected the dummies, focusing on the Head Injury Criterion (HIC). Fit the most suitable model and interpret each parameter in the model equation.

3. **Question 3:**  
   The data contains childhood growth data on 602 children from Westmead Children’s Hospital. Use a marginal (GEE) model to model the mean height of children across age, adjusting for their sex.
